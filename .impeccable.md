## Design Context

### Users
Thai hospital staff and administrators scheduling annual personnel health checks, importing appointments, checking attendance, and producing fiscal-year reports.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, dependable. The user approved a light deep-green and white direction with readable Thai typography and compact tables.

### Aesthetic Direction
Deep forest navigation, pale green-tinted work surfaces, restrained borders, Noto Sans Thai headings and Sarabun body. A useful scheduling workspace, without decorative illustrations. Status has text as well as color.

### Design Principles
- Put fiscal year, filters, and appointments before explanatory content.
- Keep personal information behind authenticated, scoped APIs.
- Make preview and confirmation explicit for imports.
- Preserve readable tables and keyboard-accessible forms on small screens.
- Show honest empty/error states; never invent staff or completion statistics.
